This course prepares leaders to hone the critical thinking skills of their entire organization. Learn how to upgrade critical thinking to avoid deceiving fallacies, spot misleading cognitive biases, craft better arguments, hone judgment, and improve decision-making. Instructor Becki Saltzman teaches skills that will improve how your company or team innovates, tackles challenges, and responds to change.

<ul>
  <li>Recognize the right moments to apply critical thinking questions.</li>
  <li>Identify statements that reflect slow, deliberate thinking.</li>
  <li>Explain the three conditions needed to trust one's own intuition.</li>
  <li>Understand why it's important to spot and avoid logical fallacies.</li>
  <li>Identify the three types of questions that spark curiosity and fuel critical thinking.</li>
</ul>
By the end of the course, learners are equipped to think more clearly, question assumptions more effectively, and make better-informed decisions — both individually and as part of a team.
