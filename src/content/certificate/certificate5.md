This certification course from AI Singapore, delivered as part of the AI Opportunity Fund: Asia-Pacific initiative led by AVPN with support from Google.org and ADB, introduces the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in a clear, practical way. It covers the relationship between AI and ML, how generative AI tools work, and how to use them responsibly and effectively in everyday work.

The course walks through real-world case studies, such as Jiva's use of AI to diagnose crop diseases for smallholder farmers and RefiBird's use of AI to sort textiles, to show how AI concepts translate into practical applications across different industries. It also explores key risks like hallucinations, data bias, and systemic bias, along with frameworks for human oversight, including Human-in-the-Loop, Human-on-the-Loop, and Human-in-Command roles.

<ul>
  <li>Understand the relationship between AI, Machine Learning, and generative AI, and how these technologies are applied in the workplace.</li>
  <li>Recognise common AI limitations such as hallucinations, knowledge cutoffs, data bias, and systemic bias, and understand their causes.</li>
  <li>Apply prompt engineering techniques, including zero-shot and few-shot prompting, to get clear and effective outputs from AI tools.</li>
  <li>Identify appropriate human oversight models, such as Human-in-the-Loop and Human-in-Command, to ensure responsible AI use.</li>
  <li>Evaluate potential harms of AI systems, including allocative, representational, quality of service, and social system harms.</li>
  <li>Apply a people-first, security-conscious approach when adopting AI tools in a professional setting.</li>
</ul>
By the end of the course, learners gain a solid foundation in using AI responsibly and effectively as an everyday digital skill, and are awarded an official certificate of completion from AI Singapore.
